# 32bit-Spacer Marketing Plan

## Positioning
A retro-futuristic space roguelike with deep systems, procedural exploration, and player-driven strategy.

## Core Channels
- GitHub/devlogs: build transparency and technical credibility.
- X/Twitter: gameplay clips, design insights, milestone progress.
- itch.io/Steam ecosystem and indie game communities.

## Content Cadence
- Weekly: one gameplay or systems clip.
- Weekly: one short design/dev insight post.
- Monthly: milestone summary and wishlist callout.

## Funnel and CTAs
- Primary CTA: Follow devlog and wishlist.
- Secondary CTA: Join playtest/community channel.
- Events: clip_view, wishlist_click, wishlist_add, demo_download, playtest_signup, return_visit.

## 90-Day Experiments
- Test combat-focused clips vs systems-focused clips for wishlist CTR.
- Test hero README framing with playable status updates.
- Test playtest callouts in weekly updates.
