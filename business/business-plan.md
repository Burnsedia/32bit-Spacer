# 32bit-Spacer Business Plan

## Product Role
32bit-Spacer is an open game IP project with devlog-led community growth and long-horizon monetization.

## ICP
- Primary: indie players who enjoy systems-heavy space roguelike experiences.
- Secondary: creators and developers interested in game systems and modding.

## FOSS and Monetization Boundary
- Free forever: open development code, devlogs, community participation.
- Paid: packaged releases, optional assets/soundtrack, supporter access perks.

## Business Model
- Build community trust in public.
- Convert engaged audience via wishlist, release purchases, and supporter channels.

## Primary KPI
- Monthly net wishlist growth.

## 90-Day Objectives
- Run one structured playtest cycle.
- Increase wishlist conversion from devlog traffic.
- Publish monthly milestone updates with measurable progress.
